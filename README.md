One line, 200 characters or fewer: the excuse a robot vacuum gives for
missing a spot. Deadpan, a little smug about its sensors, and the punch
lands at the end.

- `main.py` — the entrypoint. Writes the alibi to `/output/entry.txt`
  (one line, validated to 1–200 characters).

## The line

> I did not miss that spot. I ran a full diagnostic, declared it
> load-bearing dust, and left it holding the floor together. You're welcome.
